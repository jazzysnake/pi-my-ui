Variant B — “Journal”

Thesis: treat the conversation as a calm, readable timeline — each turn is a distinct card on a rail, with generous spacing and one clear thing to read at a time.

Optimises for: reading comfort and glanceability; obvious touch targets; a repeatable rhythm that makes a long answer pleasant to follow rather than dense to parse.

Trades away: density — far fewer messages per screen, more scrolling; the rail and card chrome cost vertical and horizontal room a phone pays for.
